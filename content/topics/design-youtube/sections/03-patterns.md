# Patterns
- **Progressive upload**: chunked upload with resume on failure.
- **Parallel transcoding**: split video into segments; encode in parallel.
- **CDN multi-tier**: origin → mid-tier → edge for cost optimization.
- **Quality of service**: popular content cached at edge; long-tail from origin.
- **Content fingerprinting**: detect copyright violations via hashing.
- **A/B testing**: compare recommendation algorithms for watch time.
