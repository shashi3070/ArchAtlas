# Tradeoffs
**Storage vs quality**: 4K is 10x larger than 720p; store all resolutions or on-demand?
**CDN cost vs latency**: more edge nodes = lower latency but higher cost.
**Transcoding cost vs freshness**: immediate encoding is expensive; batch is cheaper.

**When to prefer immediate encoding**: live streaming; time-sensitive content.
**When to prefer batch encoding**: VOD; cost optimization.
