# Tradeoffs
**SQL vs NoSQL**: SQL for complex queries and ACID; NoSQL for horizontal scale and flexibility.
**Synchronous vs async**: sync for simplicity; async for scalability.
**Caching vs freshness**: caching reduces latency but may serve stale data.
**Monolith vs microservices**: monolith for simplicity; microservices for team autonomy.

**When to prioritize scale**: high-traffic systems (10K+ QPS).
**When to prioritize simplicity**: early-stage products; small teams.
