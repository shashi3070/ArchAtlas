# Mistakes
- **Averaging latencies only** - p50 hides the pain; alert on p95/p99 and tail multi-hop sums.
- **Logging secrets/PII** - compliance incidents born in debug statements; scrub centrally.
- **Orphaned dashboards** - screens nobody consults during incidents; curate ruthlessly.
- **Missing correlation ids** - jumping between services without shared ids makes triage archaeology.
- **Alerting on causes instead of symptoms** - CPU alarms page before users suffer or long after; burn rates target impact directly.
