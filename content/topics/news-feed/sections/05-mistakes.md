# Common Mistakes
- **No content filtering**: inappropriate or harmful content appears in feeds.
- **Ignoring feed diversity**: only showing one content type (e.g., videos) reduces engagement.
- **Stale feeds**: feed doesn't update in real-time; users see old posts.
- **No pagination**: loading the entire feed at once is slow and wasteful.
- **Ignoring mobile**: feed is not optimized for mobile screens and bandwidth.
