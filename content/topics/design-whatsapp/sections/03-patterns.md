# Patterns
- **Connection management**: Erlang processes handle millions of lightweight connections.
- **Message queue**: per-user message queue for offline delivery.
- **Fan-out**: group messages are fanned out to all members' servers.
- **End-to-end encryption**: Signal Protocol; server stores ciphertext only.
- **Presence**: last-seen timestamp updated on connection events.
- **Read receipts**: two checkmarks (delivered) and blue checkmarks (read).
