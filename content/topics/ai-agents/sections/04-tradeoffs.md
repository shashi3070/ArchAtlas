# Tradeoffs
**Autonomy vs control**: more autonomy = faster but riskier; more control = safer but slower.
**Single-agent vs multi-agent**: single is simpler; multi-agent enables specialization.
**Planning vs reacting**: planning is more efficient for complex tasks; reacting is simpler.

**When to prefer single-agent**: simple tasks; small tool set.
**When to prefer multi-agent**: complex workflows; need for specialization and parallelism.
