# Tradeoffs
**Freshness vs cost**: crawling more frequently keeps the index fresh but increases bandwidth and compute.
**Depth vs breadth**: deep crawling discovers more from fewer sites; breadth covers more sites shallowly.
**Politeness vs speed**: strict politeness slows crawling; aggressive crawling risks IP bans.

**When to prefer depth**: focused crawlers (e.g., academic papers, specific domains).
**When to prefer breadth**: general-purpose search engines.
